# Paper Rewrite Notes

Date: 2026-07-14

## Current Story

The paper now treats `proposal_border` as the manuscript Proposed method. The method story is:

1. Captured-screen normalization fails when moving screen content is mistaken for physical screen motion.
2. The Proposed method uses the physical screen border as the primary homography cue.
3. LK/RANSAC is kept as a consistency diagnostic for content-motion conflicts, not as the main geometry driver.
4. The final quantitative run covers five capture conditions with two evaluated clips per condition.
5. Results show the Proposed method improves both annotated geometry and trajectory variation on the evaluated split.
